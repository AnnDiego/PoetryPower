Starlit Cockpit
*An Ode to the Versatile Cybertruck*

The truck hums up to desert’s rim,  
Rugged earth stretches wide and bare.

Reflected in stainless silver gleam

Picnic sprawls in the truck’s wide bed,

You leap out, launch a hobby flare.
It arcs high, smoke glows in starry light,
Your private launch, my heart ignites.

Chill bites, blankets too thin to warm,  
We climb to the cab, seeking heat.  
Soft touchscreen glows, our modern muse,  
Your low whisper draws my breath.

With light dim, you recite a clumsy rhyme,  
A giggle slips from my pursed lips.  
This cockpit, our world, close and warm,  
The only stars now in our eyes.
