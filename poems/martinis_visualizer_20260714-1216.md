Martinis

We sip martinis,
yours neat,
mine dirty—
already telling.

Brine nips at my tongue,
stoking dreams
of urgent whispers at lobes
and the parting of lips.

Your proper drink
paints your lips with gin alone
and they glisten. 
I’m shaken.
And your smile says 
you are stirred.
