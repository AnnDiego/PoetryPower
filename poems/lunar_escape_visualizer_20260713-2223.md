## Source poem

```
Lunar Escape

Not for adventure
(though it’s the closest I’ll ever come to a quest)

Not to witness her beauty
(her colorless chalk surface holds none)

Not for fame
(I’m only going where man has already been)

But I would take that long trip anyway
just to turn our broad gap physical—
the two hundred thousand miles between me and you.

I will feel the distance
amplified in my veins,
cold and distant, fittingly.

At home with my heart’s icy twin.

Like me, she lies in apogee,
slowly pulling away from you.
```
